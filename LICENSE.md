## Data & privacy

This software connects to a Firebase project operated on behalf of Yorkshire
International College to store student, attendance, and fee records. Access
is restricted to authorized staff accounts (Super Admin, Administrator, and
Coach roles); a separate public lookup page allows students/parents to view
only their own attendance and fee status using a Student ID and PIN issued by
the school.
