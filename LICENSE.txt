Yorkshire International College to store student, attendance, and fee
records. Access is restricted to authorized staff accounts (Super Admin,
Administrator, and Coach roles). A separate public lookup page allows
students/parents to view only their own attendance and fee status using a
Student ID and PIN issued by the school.
